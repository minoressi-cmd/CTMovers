FAQ

Stuff people usually ask.

Didn't cover yours? Text us.

How far will you travel?

We cover our town and the ones around it, roughly a 20-minute drive. If you're a little farther, ask. We'll usually make it work for a bigger job.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo, Zelle, or Apple Pay when the job's done. For bigger jobs we might ask for half up front to cover rentals.

Do you bring your own equipment?

Dollies, straps, moving blankets, basic tools, trash bags. If a job needs something special, like a tent or a truck rental, we'll sort that out in the quote.

How much notice do you need?

A few days is ideal, especially for weekends. Same-day is possible if we're free, so it never hurts to ask.

What if it rains?

Indoor jobs happen no matter what. For outdoor stuff we'll text you the morning of and reschedule if it's bad. No charge to move it.

Can I request specific people?

Yep. If you liked who came last time, tell us and we'll send them again if they're around.
